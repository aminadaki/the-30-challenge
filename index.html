The 30
Challenge

30 things to try, learn, discover and remember between 30 and 40. Your first one starts in Scotland.

The idea

This is a list of 30 things we thought would make your thirties a little more interesting. There’s no order to follow and no deadline — just pick one whenever it feels like a good time.

Your progress

There are no deadlines, no points and absolutely no failing. When you complete one, save the date and a little memory. Ten years from now, the ticks will have stories behind them.
